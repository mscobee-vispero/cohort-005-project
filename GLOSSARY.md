# Cadence

An online course platform where Students buy courses and work through their lessons. Students and the team use the same terms: emails and screens name these things exactly as they are named here.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
Someone who takes courses on the platform.
_Avoid_: Learner, user (when the role is meant)

### Buying

**Purchase**:
One payment for a course, recording what was paid and where the buyer was.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**List Price**:
The price an instructor sets on a course, before any discount.
_Avoid_: Full price, base price

**PPP Price**:
A List Price lowered by a purchasing-power discount based on the buyer's country.
_Avoid_: Regional price, discounted price

**Team Seat**:
One place on a team's Purchase, which a single Student can claim to take the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence

**Seat Code**:
The code a Student enters to claim a Team Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon, coupon code

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, in their own words, to get their money back for a Purchase. Someone reviews it by hand and approves or declines it, unless the buyer withdraws it first. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request nobody has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request that has been accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request that has been turned down. It has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Withdrawn Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the buyer took back before it was decided. It has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, Closed, Declined

**Refund**:
The amount paid on a Purchase, returned when a Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund that has been approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (on its own, or for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Refunded Purchase**:
A Purchase whose Refund has been issued. The buyer no longer owns the course, its unclaimed Team Seats can't be claimed, and no new Refund Request can be raised on it.

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period
